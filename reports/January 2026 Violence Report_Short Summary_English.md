# **January 2026: Bangladesh Monthly Violence Report**

**Publication date:** February 2, 2026

**Source:** [ViolenceTracker.org](http://violencetracker.org) 

**Reporting period:** January 1 \- January 31, 2026

## **1\. Summary**

The overall security situation in Bangladesh has seen significant instability in January 2026\. ViolenceTracker.org's automated monitoring system has analyzed a total of 29,951 news stories from the country's leading media outlets over the past month. Of these, 470 individual incidents of violence have been identified.

**161 people were killed** and **1,010 injured** in violence across the country in January . Analysis of the data shows that while 'Criminal Violence' tops the list in terms of the number of incidents, 'Political Violence' accounts for the highest number of injuries, which is a major threat to public safety.

## **7\. Conclusion and recommendations**

Data from January 2026 shows that more people are being killed in criminal activities than in political conflicts. However, the large number of people injured due to political reasons and the high level of violence in districts like Faridpur and Dhaka indicate a deterioration in law and order. The situation may deteriorate further in the coming months if mob justice and isolated cocktail explosions are not strictly suppressed.

**ViolenceTracker.org** is calling on policymakers and law enforcement agencies to monitor high-risk districts (especially Dhaka, Chittagong, Faridpur) and for political parties to prevent their activists from resorting to violence.
